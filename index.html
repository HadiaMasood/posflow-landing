Platform Capabilities

Built for Scale, Speed, and Reliability

Comprehensive tools designed to streamline checkout, automate inventory management, and unify business reporting.

NFC & RFID Offline Payment Engine

Our core innovation. Accept contactless smartphone taps (Apple Pay, Google Pay, JazzCash) and RFID cards even during total internet disconnects. Transactions are encrypted locally and safely queued in hardware memory.

Pakistan Payment Gateways

Pre-integrated with JazzCash, Easypaisa, HBL, Meezan Pay, and international gateways. Process transactions at competitive transparent rates.

End-to-End Encryption

PCI-DSS compliant hardware-level encryption safeguards merchant credentials and cardholder data across all processing states.

Automated Inventory Management

Real-time stock deduction on checkout. Automated low-stock thresholds prevent stockouts on key catalog items.

Role-Based Staff Management

Granular authorization controls for cashiers, store supervisors, and finance teams with complete action audit logging.

Multi-Branch & Multi-Location Management

Centralized administration for all physical stores, delivery kitchens, pop-up kiosks, and event registers from a single management console. Real-time inter-branch stock transfers and consolidated P&L.

Direct Accounting Synchronization

Automatic daily reconciliation with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and custom accounting suites without manual data entry.

Custom CSV & Tax Exports

Generate audit-ready revenue reports, tax breakdown summaries, and hourly sales analytics with a single click.

24/7 Local Support Team

Dedicated Urdu + English technical support, priority direct phone routing, and on-site setup assistance across Pakistan.
